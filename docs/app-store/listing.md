**Description** (3091/4000):

Hablo teaches Spanish one small step at a time, from your very first "hola" to C2. It is free, with no ads, no account and no subscription.

Most people don't quit Spanish because it is too hard. They quit because things move too fast, or the rules are never explained. Hablo is built the other way around: nothing appears before it has been explained, and every explanation is in your own language.

EXPLAINED IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Choose English, Italian, Ukrainian, German, French, Portuguese or Chinese. Every rule, tip and translation in the course is written in that language, so you always understand why Spanish works the way it does.

NOTHING COMES OUT OF NOWHERE
Each lesson opens with the new words, matched to their translation. Then one idea, two exercises, the next idea, two exercises, and a mix at the end. No block has more than 15 questions, so a lesson fits into a few quiet minutes.

A FULL COURSE, A0 TO C2
56 units across 8 levels: Arranque, Motor, Memoria, Historia, Fluidez, Matiz, Dominio and Maestría. Your path is a calm road of numbered units, so you always know where you are and what comes next. Each unit ends with a 10-question test. Already know some Spanish? Pass the test and skip ahead.

VERBS YOU CAN SEE
In every verb table the stem is blue and the ending is orange: habl + o, habl + as, habl + a. Conjugation stops being a list to memorise and becomes a set of building blocks. Review any table in the Lab whenever you need it.

THE LAB: 14 GAMES
Practise what you have learned, never anything you haven't. Games unlock as you progress and use the words and sentences from your own lessons:
- Parejas: match each verb form to its person.
- Contrarreloj: 60 seconds, as many correct verb forms as you can.
- ¿Qué tiempo es?: name the tense of a verb form.
- Máquina del tiempo: move a verb into another tense and write it.
- Parejas de palabras: match the words you have learned to their Spanish.
- Dilo en español: see a word in your language and say it in Spanish.
- Ordena la frase: put a sentence from your lessons back in order.
- Dictado: hear a sentence and write it. Tap twice to hear it slowly.
- Caza de errores: find the wrong verb in a sentence.
- Carrera de verbos: write whole verb tables against the clock and beat your best time.
- ¿Qué significa?: hear a Spanish word and pick what it means.
- ¿El o la?: quick rounds on the gender of the nouns you know.
- Contrarios: find the opposite, like alto and bajo.
- Termina el refrán: finish real Spanish sayings, with word-for-word translations and their meaning.

LISTEN AND SPEAK
Sentences are read aloud by a natural voice. In Speak, you hear a sentence from your finished lessons, say it out loud and see which words came through clearly.

YOUR NOTES, ALWAYS AT HAND
Every explanation and every word you have met is saved in Notes, unit by unit, so you can look something up at any moment.

REAL, EVERYDAY SPANISH
Natural sentences about your life, your home, your city and your work. No cartoon characters, no scripted dialogues.

A HABIT WITHOUT PRESSURE
A quiet streak shows the days you have practised. Turn on a daily reminder if you like, at the time you choose, and Hablo won't remind you on days you have already studied. There are hearts, but they never block a lesson.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your iPhone.

Hablo means "I speak". Open it for ten minutes a day, go slowly, and let the Spanish stick.
