**Promotional text** (170):
Learn Spanish slowly and for real: every word is explained before you use it, in your own language. Play with your verbs in Contrarreloj and Caza de errores.

**Description**:
HablaBase teaches Spanish one small step at a time, from your very first words to C1.

- Nothing comes out of nowhere. Every new word is shown and matched before any exercise uses it, and every rule is explained in your own language: English, Italian, Ukrainian, German, French, Portuguese or Chinese.
- Short lessons. One idea, two exercises, the next idea, two exercises, then a mix. Never more than 15 questions.
- Verbs you can see. Stems in blue, endings in orange: conjugation becomes a set of building blocks.
- Real Spanish. Everyday sentences about your life, your city and your work, read aloud by a natural voice.
- Already know some Spanish? Pass a 10-question unit test and skip ahead.
- The verb Lab. Review your tables and play: match verbs to persons, beat the clock in Contrarreloj, and find the wrong verb in sentences from your own lessons in Caza de errores.
- Speak. Say sentences aloud and see which words were understood.
- Calm by design. No ads, no account, no pressure: hearts never block a lesson.
- Private. Everything stays on your iPhone. We collect no data.
